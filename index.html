Positioning

The last mile of a translational medical ecosystem.

TAM Global builds an integrated system for discovery, validation, and delivery. TAM Pharmacy is where that system reaches the patient — the point at which a protocol stops being research and becomes a prescription somebody actually takes.

How It Works

Four steps, each one signed for.

Nothing in this sequence is automated away. Every stage has a named person accountable for it, and the record follows the prescription.

01

Prescription received

Your prescriber sends the order by e-prescribe, fax, or phone. We confirm patient details, allergies, and current medications before it enters the queue.

02

Clinical review

A pharmacist reviews the formulation for interactions, dose appropriateness, and stability. If something doesn't reconcile, we call the prescriber — not the patient.

03

Compounded and checked

The preparation is compounded to the master formulation record, then verified against it. Ingredients, lot numbers, and beyond-use date are documented for every unit.

04

Dispensed and shipped

Counseling is offered on every new prescription. Orders ship tracked, with temperature-controlled packaging where the preparation requires it.

Standards

What we will and won't do

Prescription only

We dispense against a valid prescription from a licensed prescriber with an established patient relationship. There is no route around that, and we will not create one.

No essential copies

Compounding exists to fill gaps a manufactured product leaves. We do not compound what is commercially available in the form the patient needs.

Documented, not asserted

Ingredient source, lot, and beyond-use date are recorded for every preparation. If you ask what is in your medication and where it came from, there is an answer on file.
